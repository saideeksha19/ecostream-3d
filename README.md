\# EcoStream 3D



> An immersive 3D urban watershed digital twin for environmental risk analysis, scenario simulation, forecasting, and One Health resilience insights.



\## Overview



EcoStream 3D is an interactive digital twin designed to transform watershed observations into explainable environmental risk insights.



The platform combines environmental observations, statistical analysis, spatial risk propagation, scenario simulation, forecasting, and One Health interpretation within an immersive 3D watershed environment.



Instead of presenting environmental data as isolated charts, EcoStream 3D connects observations to their spatial context and shows how changing environmental conditions can influence watershed risk.



\## Key Features



\- Immersive 3D watershed digital twin

\- 12 watershed zones and 13 monitoring/citizen observation points

\- 3,754 deterministic environmental observations

\- Turbidity, conductivity, dissolved oxygen, water level, and temperature analysis

\- Robust anomaly detection using median/MAD-based z-scores

\- Hour-of-day seasonal correction

\- 7-day Theil–Sen trend analysis

\- Data quality and completeness assessment

\- Environmental risk scoring

\- Risk states: NORMAL, WATCH, INVESTIGATE, and EARLY WARNING

\- Spatial risk propagation across connected watershed zones

\- Scenario simulation for:

&#x20; - Heavy Urban Runoff

&#x20; - Extreme Rainfall

&#x20; - Heat Stress

\- Forecast views for +24 hours, +72 hours, and +7 days

\- Explainable evidence behind risk assessments

\- One Health environmental and community impact interpretation

\- Human-verification workflow

\- Data provenance labels including OBSERVED, DERIVED, SIMULATED, and HUMAN\_VERIFIED
